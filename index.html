      Levels: every roll and arcade game earns XP, and rarer rolls earn more. Each level makes free arcade stakes and daily wheel prizes 5% bigger, and every 10 levels adds an extra roll or ticket to the wheel. Charms can be upgraded up to level 5. Each level gives points, gold and a ticket, with 3 tickets every 5th level. Gold comes from rare badges (Rare 2, Epic 8, Legendary 30) and is spent in the Vault, which opens once every upgrade is maxed. Each roll has six slots that roll left to right. A leading slot has a 1 in 11 chance to vanish, and if it does the next one can vanish too. The last slot always lands a digit. Every badge multiplies your score by (1 ÷ its chance) to the power of 0.75, so rare badges count for a lot. Rolls happen on the server, so nobody can fake one.
